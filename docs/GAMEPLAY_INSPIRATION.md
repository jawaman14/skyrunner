# Gameplay inspiration: personal skill, organisation and a visible world

Research note, 8 October 2026. Supplements [EMPIRE_MILESTONE.md](EMPIRE_MILESTONE.md); it does not replace its delivery sequence or authorise every proposed mechanic. Official feature descriptions establish mechanics, not why every player enjoys them. Enjoyment explanations below are design hypotheses to test in Skyrunner. No fresh hands-on comparison session was performed.

## Mount & Blade family

| Game / expansion | Design lesson inferred from its documented mechanics | Application to Skyrunner |
|---|---|---|
| Original Mount & Blade | Personal combat and movement skill coexist with progression from lone adventurer to commander/property owner. The player's changing scale creates a personal story. | Retain valuable personal flying throughout the empire phase. A good pilot can intervene in a shipment or rescue operation rather than merely opening a management menu. |
| Warband | Combat, kingdom building and faction interaction connect field success with wider ambitions. | Make existing turf, wages and crew survival matter to the next operation. Explain why defending a district protects an investment. |
| With Fire & Sword | Firearms, soldier equipment choices and faction quest lines give forces and allegiances different practical roles. | Explain current weapon range, ammunition, vehicle and squad trade-offs before expanding the arsenal. Keep risk legible through battle accounts. |
| Napoleonic Wars | Multiplayer roles such as artillery, engineers and musicians give players distinct contributions to a shared battle. | Give pilot, dispatcher, quartermaster and ground commander complementary work and acknowledged handoffs. Avoid making non-pilot seats passive spectators. |
| Viking Conquest / Reforged Edition | Historical places, evolving events and a consequential story frame the sandbox with identity. | Make Costa Brava districts, contacts and existing campaign stages recognisable. Use period landmarks and attributable faction responses. |
| Bannerlord | A sandbox campaign combines personal action, command and economy; increasing scale changes the player's responsibilities. | Reward progression with comprehensible delegation and better organisation tools, while preserving personal intervention. |
| War Sails | Water transport, supply, storms and blockades connect geography with campaign choices. | Make existing aircraft, road and boat access strategically different. Do not add naval combat to obtain this benefit. |

## Schedule I

The documented progression connects personal manufacture/distribution, properties, employees, automated chains, pressure and co-op. My inference: automation is rewarding because the player first understands the work it removes, and can see the operation growing.

Adapt the progression structure to existing cargo/logistics: complete a run personally, understand inventory and cash handling, delegate that known task, then resolve exceptions. Show a worker's assignment, carried goods, destination and blocked reason. Let operational locations display authoritative activity and modest stock indicators. Separate decorative life from actual simulation ownership. Avoid adding repetitive handling minigames or production recipes merely to resemble this game.

Source: [TVGS press kit](https://www.scheduleonegame.com/presskit/schedule-one/index.html).

## Cities: Skylines and II

The original connects transport, services, economy and district policies. The sequel's traffic diary describes route costs, freight, parking and diagnostic views. My inference: enjoyment comes partly from seeing a problem, changing an understandable lever and observing improvement; visual city character also supports ownership.

For Skyrunner, show the existing supply network: source stock, assigned carrier, route, loading access, destination need and money outside the safe. Clicking a blocked order should highlight the known obstacle. Keep financial and territory explanations on separate useful map layers. Present reported risks with source/age/uncertainty; do not reveal hidden enemies. Borrow diagnostic clarity rather than implementing a full municipal simulator or expensive cosmetic traffic.

Sources: [Original features](https://www.paradoxinteractive.com/games/cities-skylines/about), [II traffic diary](https://www.paradoxinteractive.com/games/cities-skylines-ii/features/traffic-ai).

## Fit into the current delivery plan

1. **Reliable geography first:** finish loading/meeting connectors and profile graph searches before enabling checked truck/squad travel. Existing rebuild evidence still has 86/99 blocked loading pairs and materially increased query costs.
2. **Visible organisation:** extend existing HQ/station detail and order outcomes. Connect inventory, route, cash and district condition in a single selected-operation view; do not rebuild already completed cards.
3. **Earn delegation:** add guidance at existing campaign unlocks explaining what an assignment manages, spends and leaves to the player. Preserve four tutorial and twelve story chapters, ownership and payroll rules.
4. **Remember consequences:** use authoritative operational/battle history to retain named worker service, losses and recoveries. Rich relationships, loyalty systems and new promotion rules remain proposals, not this milestone's implementation scope.
5. **Readable physical life:** finish the coastal reference corridor, then period vehicles, crew identity, loading/parking presentation and event-driven audio/effects. Animation cannot determine arrival or inventory transfer.
6. **Shared command in co-op:** test complementary tasks, durable acknowledgements and human/AI handoff with real peers. Do not infer multiplayer quality from local protocol tests.

## Playtest hypotheses

- After one personal run and one delegated run, can a player explain the trade-off and identify an occasion to fly again?
- Can they find and explain a blocked delivery using the map, without developer assistance?
- Can they explain a wage/stock shortage and choose a corrective operation?
- Do named crew and battle accounts produce concern for losses? Record player comments; do not treat a roster view as proof of attachment.
- Can two players coordinate a shipment with useful work for both and without duplicated mutations?
- Does a setback leave a understandable recovery choice rather than repetitive travel or opaque loss?

Record completion, help requests, menu time, avoidable repetition and the player's explanations. Distinguish strategic mistakes from access/input failures. Observe early pilot, first delegation and later turf-war sessions separately. Automated seeds verify consistency and balance, not enjoyment. Keep new aircraft, extra campaign chapters, relationship simulation, finer capture districts and major combat additions deferred.
