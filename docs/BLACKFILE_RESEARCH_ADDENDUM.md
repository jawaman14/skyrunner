## Significant newer MKULTRA material

The National Security Archive's **30 October 2025** publication of the 15–18 October 1975 Church Committee staff testimony of Sidney Gottlieb is a key input not to omit:

https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/briefing-book/dnsa-intelligence/2025-10-30/top-secret-testimony-cias-mkultra-chief-50-years-later

This gives direct, qualified retrospective evidence on historical behavioural research, safehouses, MKDELTA, the Frank Olson case and how reports of success should be evaluated. The Archive also noted different **7–9 October 1975** transcripts remained unreleased as of the 2025 publication. Do **not** substitute claims of effective programmable 'mind control' for what the transcripts actually show.
